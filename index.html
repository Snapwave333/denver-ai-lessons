02 / The session

No course to finish.
Just work to move forward.

For people consulting on AI, or preparing to start. You keep the client relationship and do the work. I help you think it through.

  1. Tell me what you're working on.

    Use the signup form to tell me about your project and choose a requested time. The first 15-minute chat is free.

  2. Find a time and bring your material.

    Online sessions use Google Meet. Your request is confirmed only when you receive a calendar invitation. In-person sessions in the Denver area are arranged separately.

  3. Work together for 60 minutes.

    Focus on your project, test your approach, and identify your next steps. Pay $50 when the session is complete.

03 / A few things to know

Before we meet.

Training, rather than outsourced consulting.

We work on your methods and deliverables together. I don't take over your client engagement. This isn't a certification or a credential.

Keep client information private.

Bring sample or redacted material. We can work on the structure and thinking without sharing confidential data.

One session is fine.

There's no package to buy and nothing due up front. Book another session if you want to keep working.

Remote or local.

Google Meet sessions are available from anywhere. In-person sessions cover Denver and surrounding communities, including Aurora, Lakewood, Arvada, Westminster, Thornton, Centennial, Littleton, Englewood and Golden.
