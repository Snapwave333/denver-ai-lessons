What we work on

Questions

No. Sessions are training and working practice on your own methods and deliverables. You keep the client relationship and the work.

Can I share a client's confidential data?

Please don't. Use sample or redacted material so we can work on the structure and the thinking without exposing anything private.

Is this a certification?

No. It is private, practical training. There is no exam or credential.

Where do the sessions happen?

Online over Zoom from anywhere, or in person at a public place around the Denver area that works for both of us.

When do I pay?

After the session is complete. Nothing up front.

Can I book more than one session?

Yes. You can book a single session or a few over the week. Just ask when you reach out.
